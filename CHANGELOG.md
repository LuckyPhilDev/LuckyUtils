## [1.4.0] - 2026-06-13

### Added
- New reusable search input for dependent addons, with a placeholder hint and a one-click clear button.

## [1.3.1] - 2026-06-06

### Fixed
- Settings panels now show the correct on or off state in the info panel when a setting is displayed without hovering over it.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-06-05

### Fixed
- Minimap buttons now sit correctly on the edge of the minimap at any size, instead of sometimes hiding behind a larger minimap.
- Removed a black square that could appear when hovering a minimap button.

### Improved
- Minimap buttons can now be repositioned with a simple click and drag, no need to hold Shift.
